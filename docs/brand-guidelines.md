# Alturascope Brand Guidelines

## Brand Name
**ALTURASCOPE** — always uppercase in logo usage, with wide letter-spacing.

---

## Colour Palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Usage |
|------|------|-----|-------|
| Primary Dark | Navy | `#0B1F3A` | Backgrounds, headings, body text |
| Secondary Dark | Navy Light | `#162D4E` | Alternate dark sections |
| Accent | Gold | `#C9A84C` | Labels, icons, highlights, dividers, button fills |
| Light Background | Off-White | `#F7F5F1` | Alternate light sections, stats bars |
| Secondary Text | Mid-Grey | `#6B7280` | Body copy on light backgrounds |
| Borders | Border | `#E5E0D8` | Subtle dividers, card edges |

### Colour Usage Rules
- **Dark sections**: Navy background, white or off-white text (with opacity variations: 90%, 70%, 65%, 60%, 40%)
- **Light sections**: White or off-white background, navy headings, mid-grey body text
- **Gold is always the accent** — used sparingly for labels, icon colour, top-border highlights on cards, horizontal divider lines, and primary CTA buttons
- **No gradients** — the brand uses solid colours only
- **Image overlays**: Navy at 72% opacity (`rgba(11, 31, 58, 0.72)`) over photography in hero sections

## Typography

### Font
**DM Sans** (Google Fonts) — the only typeface used across the entire brand.

Weights: Light (300), Regular (400), Medium (500)

### Type Scale

| Element | Size | Weight | Extras |
|---------|------|--------|--------|
| Logo | 20px | Light (300) | Uppercase, wide letter-spacing (0.125em) |
| Hero H1 | 57.6px (desktop) / 28.8px (mobile) | Light (300) | Line-height 1.15 |
| Section H2 | 32–38px | Light (300) | Line-height 1.15 |
| Subsection H3 | 17.6–19.2px | Medium (500) | Line-height 1.15 |
| Body text | 14.4–16.8px | Light (300) or Regular (400) | Line-height 1.7 |
| Labels/Tags | 11.2–12px | Medium (500) | **Uppercase, letter-spacing 0.15em, gold colour** |
| Navigation | 14px | Medium (500) | Letter-spacing 0.05em |
| Small/Footer text | 12.8px | Regular (400) | — |

### Key Typography Principles
- **Headings are light-weight (300)** — this gives the brand its clean, architectural feel
- **Labels and interactive elements use medium (500)** — for clarity at small sizes
- **Generous line-height on body text (1.7)** — spacious, easy to read
- **Tight line-height on headings (1.15)** — compact, impactful
- **Bold (600/700) is never used**

## Logo

- **Text-only logotype**: "ALTURASCOPE" set in DM Sans Light
- Uppercase, with wide tracking (letter-spacing 0.125em)
- **On dark backgrounds**: White
- **On light backgrounds**: Navy (#0B1F3A)
- No icon, no symbol — the wordmark is the logo

---

## Visual Style

### Overall Aesthetic
Minimal, professional, and architecturally inspired. High contrast. Generous whitespace. No clutter.

### Backgrounds
Alternating rhythm of:
- **White** — primary content
- **Off-white (#F7F5F1)** — statistics, credibility sections
- **Navy (#0B1F3A)** — hero sections, high-impact areas
- **Navy Light (#162D4E)** — alternate dark sections

### Photography
- Used at scale in hero sections with navy overlay
- Typically construction, site survey, and building imagery
- Images shown at reduced opacity (30–40%) on dark backgrounds

### Cards & Containers
- White background with subtle border (`#E5E0D8`)
- Optional gold top-border accent (3px)
- Subtle shadow: `0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.06)`
- Minimal border-radius (4px)
- Hover: border transitions to gold

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Gold background, navy text, **square corners** (no border-radius), medium weight
- **Secondary**: Transparent with navy border and navy text, square corners
- **Secondary on dark**: Transparent with white border (40% opacity), white text
- All buttons are square-cornered — this is a deliberate brand choice

### Decorative Elements
- Horizontal gold lines (60px wide) as section dividers
- Gold-coloured outline icons (Heroicons style, stroke-width 1.5)
- No gradients, no drop-shadows on text, no rounded buttons

---

## Spacing Principles

- Generous vertical padding between sections (80px mobile, 120px desktop)
- Content max-width: 1400px outer, 700–900px for text blocks
- Horizontal padding: 24px mobile, 40px desktop
- Consistent use of whitespace to create hierarchy and breathing room

---

## Tone of Voice

### Characteristics
- **Professional but direct** — no jargon for jargon's sake, but assumes the reader knows construction
- **Confident without being boastful** — lets the work speak
- **Specificity over vagueness** — references real measurements, real deliverables
- **Problem-solution framing** — names the pain, then shows the answer
- **Consistency is a core message** — "The brief changes. The standard doesn't."

### Key Themes
1. **Consistency** — same standard, every site, every time
2. **Completeness** — everything documented, nothing missed
3. **Usability** — deliverables structured for decisions, not for storage
4. **Professionalism** — built by construction professionals
5. **National coverage** — travel included, no regional limitations

### Voice Don'ts
- No hype or superlatives ("best in class", "world-leading")
- No exclamation marks
- No emojis in professional materials
- No overly casual language
