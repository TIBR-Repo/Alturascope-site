# Privacy Policy

**Personal QuickBooks Integration — Internal Use Only**

Last updated: 22 July 2026

This application is a private, single-user integration operated by its owner to connect their own QuickBooks Online company to tooling running on their own computer.

1. **What data is accessed.** Accounting data (customers, invoices, bills, payments, reports, and similar records) from the owner's own QuickBooks Online company, via Intuit's official API.
2. **Where data goes.** Data is transferred directly between Intuit's servers and the owner's personal computer over HTTPS. It is not sent to, stored on, or shared with any third-party service.
3. **Storage.** OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. No accounting data is retained outside QuickBooks itself except transiently, at the owner's direction, on the owner's own machine.
4. **Third parties.** No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to anyone.
5. **Cookies and tracking.** None.
6. **Revocation.** Access can be revoked at any time from Intuit's connected-apps page, which invalidates the application's tokens.

Questions: contact the owner of this application.
